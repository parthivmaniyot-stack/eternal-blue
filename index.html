A sustainable rainwater filtration and reuse system designed for Adelaide Botanic High School.

DISCOVER OUR SYSTEM ↓

Rain should be a resource, not runoff.

When rain hits hard surfaces around a school, it rushes away instead of soaking into the ground. Along the way, it can collect sediment, litter and pollution.

🌧️

Fast stormwater runoff

Concrete and paving leave little space for water to slow down or soak in.

〰️

Sediment & pollution

Water can carry dirt, leaves and contaminants into the wider drainage system.

💧

Water is wasted

Useful rainwater is sent away instead of supporting gardens and cleaning.

Our Solution

Rainwater is captured, filtered, stored and reused through a simple multi-stage system.

🌧️

1. Capture

Permeable interlocking concrete pavers allow rainwater to enter the system.

💧

2. Filter

Multiple filtration layers progressively remove sediment and impurities.

🛢️

3. Store

Filtered water collects in a coated galvanised steel storage tank.

♻️

4. Reuse

A manual pump provides access to stored water without electricity.

Why a layered filter?

Each material has one job. Together, they slow down water and remove different kinds of unwanted material before storage.

Maintenance

Regular maintenance keeps the Eternal Blue system clean, efficient and reliable for long-term water reuse.

🧹
WEEKLY

Cleaning & Debris Removal

Sweep the pavers and remove leaves, rubbish and dirt. Keep the surrounding area clean to reduce dust and sediment entering the system.

🔎
MONTHLY

System Inspection

Inspect the pavers for blockages or damage, check water flow and inspect the tank, overflow system, pump and suction pipe.

🪨
EVERY 6 MONTHS

Filter & Sediment Check

Inspect for sediment buildup and check accessible filtration layers for clogging or reduced water flow.

🔧
AS REQUIRED

Repairs & Cleaning

Remove debris or sediment if water flow slows. Repair or replace damaged components and clean the tank when needed.

💙
WHY IT MATTERS

Clean System → Efficient Filtration → Reliable Reuse

Regular maintenance prevents blockages, maintains water flow and extends the life of the system.
